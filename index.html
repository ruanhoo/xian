    Little tips for big adventures

    🌸

    When to go

    Spring and autumn are mild and sunny. Summer is hot, winter is chilly but quiet.

    🚇

    Getting around

    The metro is fast and cheap inside the city. Use taxi or ride-hailing for day trips to the Terracotta Army.

    🎟️

    Tickets

    Major museums and sites often need online reservations. Keep your passport handy.

    📱

    Paying

    Alipay and WeChat Pay run almost everywhere. Link a foreign card before you arrive and carry a little cash.
